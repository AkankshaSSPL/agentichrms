# Exit and Resignation Policy

## Resignation Process
Employees who wish to resign are requested to provide a written resignation letter to their 
manager, with a copy to HR. The notice period is [X] weeks as per employment contract.

## Exit Interview
HR will schedule an exit interview to understand the reasons for leaving and to gather feedback 
to improve our workplace. Participation is voluntary but encouraged.

## Clearance and Final Settlement
Before the last working day, employees must complete clearance procedures (return company 
property, settle dues, etc.). Final settlement will be processed within [Y] days after exit.

## Re‑hire Eligibility
Employees who leave on good terms may be eligible for re‑hire after a cooling‑off period.

For any questions, please contact your HR representative.

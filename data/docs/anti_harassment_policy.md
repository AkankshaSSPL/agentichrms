# Anti-Harassment and POSH Policy

## Policy Statement
We are committed to providing a workplace free from harassment, discrimination, and retaliation. 
The Company prohibits all forms of harassment, including but not limited to sexual harassment, 
bullying, and any conduct that creates a hostile work environment.

## POSH (Prevention of Sexual Harassment) Complaint Process
If you wish to raise a complaint under the POSH Act (or similar laws), please contact the 
Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) confidentially. You may also speak to a trusted manager 
or HR representative. All complaints will be investigated promptly and impartially.

## Confidentiality
The Company ensures confidentiality throughout the complaint process. No retaliation will be 
tolerated against anyone who reports concerns in good faith.
